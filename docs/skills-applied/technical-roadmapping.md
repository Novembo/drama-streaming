# Technical Roadmapping

## Domain playbook

Inventory product commitments, operational pain, security exposure, dependency risk, lifecycle deadlines and developer friction. Estimate impact, urgency, confidence, effort and opportunity cost; make uncertainty explicit. Sequence enabling work before dependent delivery. Reserve capacity for reliability, upgrades and debt. Define outcome metrics and decision checkpoints, not only output milestones. Reprioritize from evidence and communicate trade-offs.

## Practical example

Prioritize a database upgrade before a feature that depends on unsupported extensions; measure incident rate and deployment lead time.

## Review checklist

Check strategic alignment, dependencies, capacity, risk reduction, measurable outcomes and review cadence.

## Standards

Delivery workflow, quality gates and the completion report: follow `senior-engineering-standards` (included in the `core` profile).
